## 1. Ijara Muntahia Bittamleek

SLC supports Ijara Muntahia Bittamleek for residential property. The bank owns the property and leases it to the customer, and ownership passes to the customer at the end of the lease by gift or sale. Rental can be fixed or linked to a benchmark with periodic review.

## 2. Diminishing Musharaka

SLC supports Diminishing Musharaka, in which the bank and the customer co-own the property and the customer buys the bank's share in instalments. The bank's rental income falls as the customer's share grows. Each purchase of a unit is recorded as a separate transaction.

## 3. Limits

Maximum tenor and financing-to-value limits are configured for each product by the bank.
