## Terms

- Murabaha: Sale of an asset at disclosed cost plus an agreed mark-up, with payment deferred. Also referred to as: cost-plus sale, deferred payment sale, MPO, murabahah.
- Ijara: Lease of an asset where the lessor keeps ownership; IMB ends with transfer of ownership. Also referred to as: lease, ijarah, leasing, IMB, ijara muntahia bittamleek.
- Musharaka: Partnership financing with shared profit and loss. Also referred to as: partnership, joint venture, diminishing musharaka, DM, declining partnership.
- Mudaraba: Capital provider and manager share profit at an agreed ratio; the capital provider bears loss. Also referred to as: investment account, profit sharing, PSR, mudarabah.
- Tawarruq: Purchase of a commodity on deferred terms and sale to a third party to raise cash. Also referred to as: commodity murabaha, organised tawarruq, liquidity finance, tawarruq.
- Wakala: Agency contract where an agent invests or manages funds for a fee. Also referred to as: agency, wakalah, agency deposit.
- Sukuk: Asset-backed Shariah-compliant investment certificates. Also referred to as: islamic bond, certificates, sukuk servicing, sukuk issuance.
- Takaful: Cooperative risk-sharing, the Shariah-compliant alternative to conventional insurance. Also referred to as: islamic insurance, cooperative insurance, takaful operator.
- Ibra': Waiver of part of a debt, applied as a rebate on early settlement. Also referred to as: early settlement rebate, ibra, rebate.
- Gharamah: Late-payment charge over actual cost, directed to charity rather than recognised as income. Also referred to as: late payment charge, late fee, penalty, ta'widh, tawidh.
- Shariah Supervisory Board: Body of scholars that approves products and contracts and oversees Shariah compliance. Also referred to as: SSB, shariah board, fatwa, shariah committee.
- Purification: Segregation and donation of income that is not Shariah-compliant. Also referred to as: non-compliant income, charity account, tathir.
- Riba: Prohibited interest or excess in a loan or exchange. Also referred to as: interest, usury.
- Hijri: The Islamic lunar calendar. Also referred to as: islamic calendar, lunar calendar.
- AAOIFI: Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions. Also referred to as: accounting standards, FAS, shariah standards.
- Retail lending: Lending to individuals rather than corporates. Also referred to as: consumer financing, consumer finance, retail financing, personal financing, retail Islamic lending.
- Implementation timeline: How long a delivery project takes and the phases it runs through. Also referred to as: roll out, rollout, go-live, go live, time to implement, time to deploy, delivery timeline, project duration, duration and phases.
- Knowledge transfer: Training provided to the customer's staff. Also referred to as: staff training, user training, enablement, train-the-trainer.
- Recovery objectives: Targets for how fast and how completely service returns after an outage. Also referred to as: RTO, RPO, recovery time, recovery point, failover, region outage.
- Data localisation: Keeping data inside a given country or region. Also referred to as: data residency, in-country data, data sovereignty, data localization.
- Third-party assurance: Independent verification of security and operational controls. Also referred to as: independent audit, attestation, assurance report, SOC 2, penetration test.
