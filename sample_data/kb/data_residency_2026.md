# Data Residency and Regional Availability Note

> SAMPLE CONTENT: synthetic, not real Azentio data. Fictional product for demonstration only.

Document date: 2026-04-02 | Product version: 6.2

## 1. Generally available regions

- India: AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai).
- EU: AWS eu-central-1 (Frankfurt).

## 2. Planned regions

UAE: AWS me-central-1 is planned, with general availability targeted for Q1 2027. It is not yet available and is not a contractual commitment. Other regions are considered on request, subject to a feasibility study.

## 3. Scope of residency

Production data, backups and logs containing personal data stay in the contracted region. Telemetry that contains no personal data may be processed centrally.
