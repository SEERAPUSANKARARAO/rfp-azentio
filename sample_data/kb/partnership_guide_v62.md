## 1. Musharaka

Musharaka partnership financing shares profit at an agreed ratio and shares loss in proportion to capital contribution.

## 2. Diminishing Musharaka

In Diminishing Musharaka the bank's share declines as the customer buys units periodically. Rental is payable on the bank's remaining share. The rental rate can be fixed, or linked to the published reference rates EIBOR, SAIBOR or SOFR through configurable rate tables.

Proprietary or custom index benchmarks are not natively supported. A custom index can be used only if the customer's treasury team maintains its rate table manually. There is no automated feed for custom indices.

## 3. Mudaraba investment accounts

Mudaraba investment accounts use a profit-sharing ratio (PSR) with pool-based profit distribution and pool weightages. A profit equalisation reserve (PER) and an investment risk reserve (IRR) are supported.
