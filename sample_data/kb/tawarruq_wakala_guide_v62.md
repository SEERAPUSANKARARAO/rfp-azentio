## 1. Organised Tawarruq

MIBS orchestrates organised Tawarruq for liquidity and personal finance: purchase of a commodity by the bank, sale to the customer on deferred terms, and sale by the customer to a third party. Commodity broker connectivity is provided through configurable adapters. Reverse Tawarruq is available for deposits.

## 2. Wakala

Wakala (agency) investment deposits and Wakala-based interbank placements are supported from version 6.0. The agency fee and the expected profit rate are captured on each placement.
